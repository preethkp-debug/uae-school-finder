this area has emerged as one of the most-asked-about catchments for school-aged families in Dubai, and for good reason — the mix of new-build communities, established residential clusters and good transport links makes it a natural draw. Our database currently lists 8 schools serving children who live in this area within a 15-minute drive, spanning the full spectrum from EYFS-only nurseries to all-through schools that take students from FS1 to Year 13.

Curriculum coverage in this area

Within a 15-minute drive of this area, parents have a choice between International, MOE / Bilingual, UK. If your child's curriculum isn't available in the area itself, the next nearest school of equivalent quality is usually a 20-30 minute drive at school-run times — manageable for most families, but worth checking before you sign a lease.

Fees and value benchmarking

Fees for the schools serving this area typically range from the mid AED 20,000s to over AED 80,000 a year for the most established premium options. The best-value picks are usually the well-led mid-tier schools that score Very Good in the inspection — strong outcomes, professional facilities, fees in the AED 30-50,000 band. The premium picks are worth the spend when their cohort and university destinations are what your child needs.

Don't forget to budget the extras. Uniform, books, devices, school trips and after-school clubs typically add 10-15% on top of headline tuition. Many schools also charge a non-refundable registration fee (AED 500-2,500) and a refundable seat deposit when you accept an offer.

Transport, commute and the school run

All the schools serving this area run bus networks. The typical morning commute by car at school-run hours is 10-25 minutes within the catchment, longer if you're heading to a school in a different community. Bus subscriptions range from AED 7,000 to AED 12,000 per academic year depending on distance and door-to-door vs. shuttle stop service.

If both parents work, plan for late pickup — most schools run an after-school programme until 4:30 or 5pm and a longer aftercare service until 6pm at extra cost. Check this on the tour: it's a daily reality, not a marketing detail.

Lifestyle fit — is this area right for your family?

School choice and neighbourhood choice in the UAE are almost the same conversation. Before you commit to this area, walk the area on a Saturday morning. Is there a local park your child will actually use? A paediatric clinic within five minutes? A supermarket with the brands you want? An evening swim or weekend football club that fits your family's routine? These are the things that decide whether the school choice was the right one a year later.

Rental prices for a 3-bed family home in the catchment can swing AED 30,000-60,000 per year depending on building, age and parking — factor that into the total cost of education, because some areas charge a premium precisely because of the schools they serve.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the highest-rated school in this area?

As of the most recent inspection cycle the highest-rated school in this area in our database is the top-tier school listed above. Always check the live Schools Compared listing for the current rating — these change with every inspection cycle.

Are there British / IB / American schools in this area?

Within or close to this area you'll find International, MOE / Bilingual, UK. If your preferred curriculum isn't listed, the nearest equivalent school is typically a 20-30 minute drive in school-run traffic.

What does it cost to send one child to school in this area?

Budget AED 35,000-70,000 per year as a realistic working figure for one child at a mid-to-upper tier school in this area, before transport, uniform and extras. For a premium British or IB school the all-in number is closer to AED 90,000-120,000 by sixth form.

How early should I apply?

For the most popular schools in this area, apply 6-9 months ahead of the start date. Less oversubscribed schools are typically more flexible — 4-8 weeks ahead can work, particularly for mid-year placements.

Do the schools provide bus transport?

Every school serving this area offers a bus service. Costs typically range AED 7,000-12,000 per academic year. Confirm pick-up and drop-off points on the tour, and ask about CCTV, attendant supervision, and the routing policy on hot days and Ramadan timings.
