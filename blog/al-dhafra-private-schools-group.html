Who is this operator

this operator is one of the operator groups shaping the UAE schools landscape. Like other group operators, they run a portfolio of branded and sub-branded schools across multiple emirates, centralised on leadership development, curriculum quality assurance and procurement. For parents, the practical question isn't really whether this operator is good — it's whether the specific school in their catchment, run under the this operator umbrella, is the right fit.

The full this operator portfolio in the UAE

this operator's current UAE portfolio is best checked live on the Schools Compared directory — filter by 'Operator: this operator' for an up-to-date list with current ratings and fees.

How their schools differ from each other

Operator groups rarely treat all their schools the same. There is usually a flagship — the school with the highest fees, the most distinguished leadership, and the strongest university destinations — and then a tier of value brands aimed at mid-market families. There may also be specialist schools focused on a specific curriculum strand or age range.

Choose the this operator school that fits your family — not the brand at the top of the marketing material. The weakest school in any group's portfolio is usually worse than the strongest school of a smaller operator in the same area.

Fee benchmarking across the group

this operator's schools span the fee spectrum. The cheapest this operator school in our database charges in the affordable band; the flagship charges in the premium or ultra-premium band. Sibling discounts typically apply across the group's schools — meaning if you have one child at a value-tier school and another at a premium school, the second-child discount may still apply. Confirm this with admissions; the discount mechanics vary by group.

What parents say

Across the Schools Compared review base, common praise themes for this operator schools include consistency of approach, the professionalism of the central admissions and finance teams, and the visible investment in facilities. Common criticism themes include perceived corporate distance from the school floor, and the sense that decisions get made centrally rather than at the school level. The best {op} schools are run by heads with strong autonomy — ask, during the tour, how decisions actually get made.

How to choose between two of their schools

When two this operator schools are both within commute, compare three things: the most recent inspection report section by section (not just the headline grade), the head's tenure and trajectory (a new head can transform a school in 12-18 months, for better or worse), and the cohort fit for your child. Tour both on the same week so the comparison is fresh.

Frequently asked questions

Which this operator school is best?

There is no single 'best' this operator school — different schools in the portfolio excel at different things. Use the comparison filters on Schools Compared to narrow by curriculum, area and budget before deciding.

Which this operator school is cheapest?

this operator's most affordable school in the UAE is best identified live on Schools Compared — filter by 'Operator: this operator' and sort by fees ascending.

Is the this operator sibling discount worth it?

Sibling discounts at most operator groups range from 5-10% on the second and subsequent child. For families with multiple children, that's a meaningful annual saving — though it rarely tips the decision if the school isn't otherwise the right fit.

How do this operator schools differ from independents?

Operator-group schools benefit from centralised leadership development, procurement and quality assurance — but can feel more corporate. Independent schools have more local autonomy and a more distinctive culture, but less institutional muscle behind them. Both models produce excellent schools.

Where is the newest this operator school?

New schools open most years in fast-growing communities like Dubai South, Yas Island, Aljada and Tilal City. For the current pipeline, check the operator's announcements page or the 'New schools' filter on Schools Compared.
